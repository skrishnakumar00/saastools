🗂️ Set up your Project Tracker

Connect this dashboard to your own Google Sheet. You only need to do this once per browser.

Don't have this yet? In your Google Sheet: Extensions → Apps Script, paste the tracker backend script, then Deploy → New deployment → Web app (Execute as: Me, Who has access: Anyone) and copy the URL it gives you.

Your Sheet needs a Tasks tab at minimum (Sr No, Category, Task, Owner, Due Date, Revised Due Date, Status, Remarks). Notes, Targets, and Config tabs are optional.

🔑 Log in

This tracker is set up for multiple people/projects — log in to see your project(s).

First time logging in? Just choose any PIN — you'll use that same one every time after. Nobody else, including the sheet owner, sets or sees your PIN.
